# Product Context

My-MC SMP Realm Signal is a public, read-only Minecraft community dashboard for players who need a trustworthy answer before joining: is the server online, how many players are there, how fast is the connection, and what address should I use? It monitors two realms, supports realm switching, and exposes connection details, player activity, uptime, Discord help, rules, and configuration. The main audience is Minecraft players on phones and desktops. The product should feel calm, technical, welcoming, and more like a carefully designed utility than a generic gaming landing page.

The interface must preserve live status behavior, avoid misleading health claims, keep controls touch-friendly, and respect reduced-motion preferences. It is a dashboard, not a publishing or server-control console.
